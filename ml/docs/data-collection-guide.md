# Collecting data for a future authorized study

**REAL DATA (publicly reported cases): aggregate results only.** The present sample is news + High Court judgments. It is not a case census, a measure of all movement, or operational accuracy. For any future study, obtain authorization, a documented purpose and access rules before collection.

1. **Keep inputs private.** Place supplied files under `training_dataset/`. Keep sanitized cases, keys, models and audit files local and gitignored; never put them under `web/public/`, attach them to an issue, or upload them. Do not scrape or collect personal information. If district/date mappings are unclear or identifiers cannot be reliably stripped, stop and ask the owner.
2. **Agree on one case universe.** Define whether all documented seizures or only cases above a minimum weight are in scope. `ml/config.json` defaults to every usable case; a positive `minimum_quantity_grams` includes only known normalized weights at or above the floor. Report the count effect and unknowns and do not treat missing counts or weights as zero.
3. **Capture two dates.** Record seizure/offence date and first publication or judgment date separately. For news only, record when publication substitutes for the event date. If a judgment date is missing, do not guess. Keep undated cases out of time tests. Save the collection end date and allow late records to arrive before scoring.
4. **Audit one row per case.** Use the shared fields in `shared/schema.py` and `shared/schema.ts`; treat a syndicated report as one source event. Record conservative match decisions across court and news, including ambiguity. Preserve our keyed references privately, not raw URLs in the product. Keep seizure-only observations, explicit documented origin/destination, and infrastructure apart.
5. **Add only comparable coverage.** Fill `ml/data/real/official_counts_TEMPLATE.csv` with a source URL and retrieval date for each comparable district/year. Police and excise fields must have non-overlapping scopes, or leave them blank. Coverage ratio is the sample count divided by the official total, a rough observation proxy; official counts often include small possession cases. A partial year or unknown denominator stays unknown. Never expand annual totals into cases.
6. **Record changes in collection.** Put independently documented `start,end,scope,source_note` rows in `ml/data/real/campaigns.csv`, using a district or `Kerala` as scope. Keep source notes private. When possible also collect dated aggregate observation effort, changes in definitions, and independent comparison periods. Chart changes in publication volume and mix; a jump may reflect collection or enforcement, not harm.
7. **Review and test before use.** Run `make real`; inspect the local audit and compare models to simple forecasts for both windows. Re-scoring overlapping dates after changes is exploratory, not new validation. Use fresh future outcomes and agency-led prospective review before operational claims. Suppression and owner disclosure review are required even for aggregate release; compare repeated releases for small-cell inference risk.

This demo does not implement production access control, a data retention policy, or handling of live field reports. Practice leads are never model input.
