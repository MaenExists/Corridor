# NarcoTrace operating notes

- The current scope is in [project.md](project.md). Read [the model plan](docs/MODEL_PLAN.md) before changing forecasting and [the data research](docs/DATA_SOURCES.md) before considering any imports. Checkpoint in docs/progress.
- The repository owner has requested direct commits and pushes for completed, verified work in this workspace. Use the current branch on the configured NarcoTrace GitHub origin. Keep private inputs, reports, and model artifacts out of commits.
- Build in order from official aggregate intake to district forecasting and separate care planning, then evaluate documented district links. Use 30 June 2026 end of day IST as the proposed knowledge cutoff. Later mature observations form a separate check.
- Use official documented seizure or registration counts with compatible coverage and definitions. Court and news are a supplementary sample, not a census. Infrastructure is orientation. Never treat coverage gaps as safety. Keep effort, population, prevalence, care utilization and seizure measurements distinct.
- Keep synthetic and real tracks segregated. Work offline except dependency installs, the public basemap and explicitly requested public-source research or acquisition. Raw private data, file URLs, coordinates, identifiers and model artifacts stay local and out of logs, exports, commits and serving paths. Private reports remain gitignored pending owner disclosure review. Use no credentialed source or licensed corpus without authorized access. Public artifacts require synthetic provenance or a separately reviewed disclosure process.
- Preserve chronological evaluation, baselines, the leakage test, abstention, limitations and track/freshness notices. No individual scoring, automatic action or operational claims. Use coarse aggregates and manual review, with default small-cell threshold five for private releases. Suppression alone does not prove anonymity.
- The current synthetic demo lives in web/src/dashboard. It uses a fixed archive and seed 1729. Challenge runs the frozen browser model and reveals outcomes separately. This is a UI flow. Forecast evidence and aggregate brief features remain available. Practice forms are structured and local, with no external dispatch or training integration. Ask the Atlas searches saved local results.
- The real forecaster is ml/train/district_monthly.py. It uses the verified Excise district panel from ml/data/excise_enforcement.py. Its settings and gates were pre-registered and scored once. Do not retune them on the 2023–2024 test. Score a changed design on newly matured months. Issue only one month ahead, and after a series gap only once six consecutive months exist. Run make excise-snapshot each month because the live homepage keeps only the latest month. The where-next layer (ml/train/district_where.py) is also frozen and was scored once; check its calibration on new months. The 1 km grid forecast (ml/train/hotspot_grid.py, data from ml/data/news_incidents.py) was pre-registered and scored once on 2024 to mid-2026. Its audit shows the skill is mostly repeat localities, so always report it next to the repeat_locality_1km baseline. Treat news reports as a selected sample of reported seizures, and score changes only on months after November 2026. Article summaries, incident tables and served forecasts stay local and gitignored.
- The legacy private diagnostic workflow is explicit and local. It uses dated source CSVs and a deterministic district/place allowlist. Dates are never imputed, conflicting court dates are rejected, and only explicitly stated endpoints count. Its results cannot substitute for a complete official covered series.
- The public demo defaults to OpenStreetMap, with an archival NASA fallback and optional NASA detail. Use ?offline=1 for local geography and the bundled archive. The private aggregate view makes no tile requests.

## Run

Run **make setup && make demo** and visit **http://127.0.0.1:5173/?offline=1**. Use **make serve** for a prepared snapshot, **make test** for local checks, and **make browser-test** against a running server. **make real** is the legacy private diagnostic and never runs at demo startup. See [README](README.md) for the new aggregate intake command.
