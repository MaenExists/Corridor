## Assumptions

- Source CSVs, not Parquet/unified copies or totals, define the private news + High Court universe. Outside-state supplement has no Kerala district and isn't appended. The raw audit is `docs/progress/DATA_AUDIT_REAL.md` locally.
- Standard district aliases and a fixed place allowlist are used, not guessed matches or external geocoding. News publication and court judgment control availability; missing dates are not imputed. Equal court dates are used as supplied and flagged; reversed dates are excluded. Conservative exact-place/weight near-date matching may miss duplicates.
- Linked secondary news references have no reliable date; only dated distinct primary articles enter monthly source volume. Court routes are gated on judgment availability and require two explicit usable endpoints.
- No agency integration, official denominators or campaign dates assumed.
- Demo uses a fixed synthetic archive and seed 1729, with richer fictional documented-connection support to exercise modelling; real incidences are not replicated. The hidden activity and synthetic enforcement effort are not supplied to the model.
- Weekly Monday forecast origins, next-day inclusive targets, 21-day reporting maturity, separate method-selection and calibration windows, and 14 synthetic / 20 real final origins. The final model fit for evaluation is frozen at the initial final origin; fit after scoring is for future inference only. Adjacent 28-day windows overlap. A candidate must improve development mean error by 5% to unseat a simple baseline.
- Recent baseline uses up to 56 days; seasonal average uses historical windows near the same part of the year; same-period-last-year means 52 weeks back. Zero recorded outcomes in a covered sample are not zeros in underlying activity. Real diagnostic scores are retrospective and don't override abstention.
- Area forecasts require covered history/recent support; connection forecasts require at least 30 dated cases by default. Thin coverage and missing effort are not fabricated. Real coverage requires complete district-year windows and comparable combined official counts with source and retrieval date; the model sees only denominators retrieved by cutoff. Filled campaigns are assumed known from their supplied start date; an empty template means unavailable, not no enforcement.
- Private outputs suppress small cells (default five); distinct cases, not repeated overlapping windows, determine support. The browser rechecks and asks for review. These measures don't prove anonymity. The web server denies private filesystem paths and its public artifacts require synthetic provenance.
- Companion workspace review adopted the useful replay, baseline-challenger rule and monitoring ideas, plus licensed simplified boundaries and a reduced GeoNames gazetteer. We excluded exported real rows, online connectors, source assertions, route planners and next-hop hypotheses. Infrastructure remains a separate local file and a fictional visual example, never a model label.
- Local role/key handling is demo-only. Practice leads are structured, with no contact, allegation or free text. Brief exports contain structured edits and aggregates as of export, not case records. RSS and Atom fixtures only; cue usefulness is unvalidated and the batch does not retrain.
- The active entry is `web/src/dashboard/`, matching the two dashboard references without inventing live operations or confidence. It reuses the forecast lab, place profiles and evidence library from `web/src/companion/`. No companion case/model data packs, hosted authentication, external geocoding or AI connectors were imported. Ask the Atlas is an offline lookup.
- `make replay` reconstructs per-place predictions for the saved synthetic holdout using the same frozen fit, without re-scoring or modifying the evaluation. The real track has no individual replay rows.
- The overview summarizes the supplied synthetic archive as of its cutoff; the final month in the chart is flagged when partial. Forecast support is the share of district forecasts issued, not accuracy or operational readiness. A reporting-change flag is a ≥25% and ≥3 count increase across two 28-day slices, not a verified trend. Scenario outputs are arithmetic on reporting or local review, not causal effects.
- The second layout has structured, local practice reporting, receipt-only state checks, and key-gated structured analyst notes. It does not accept narratives, contacts or media, promise anonymity, or contact authorities.
