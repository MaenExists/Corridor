Three failures we found

Today the system reacts. It never sees ahead.

01 · AFTER THE FACT

Enforcement learns where only after a seizure.

Patrol and raid planning leans on memory and tips. Nothing ranks next month's places, or says how confident that ranking is.

02 · DATA GOES DARK

Official numbers arrive late, or stop.

Excise stopped uploading monthly statements in October 2024. The gap lasted nearly two years. A plan built on public data was flying blind.

Model 2 · Grid forecast v3.1

Which square kilometre, in the next four weeks?

Our key insight: a forecast of reported seizures must model how reports are located and covered. Only 5,754 of 46,057 squares can ever receive a located report. Our first model wasted 270 of its 461 picks outside them.

What drives the ranker (drop when shuffled)
461 squares = 1% of KeralaLightGBM ranker over each square's history, nearby activity, an ETAS self-exciting process, news coverage, surroundings and season. Refit every 13 weeks.
How we keep ourselves honest

We froze time to test ourselves.

Pre-registered. Settings and pass/fail gates were written down before any test score.

Scored once. No retuning on a window that has already been scored.

Chronological. Never trained on the future. A leakage test is built in.

Abstains. A forecast is withheld, with a reason, when the data is stale.

Innovation

Five things nobody had built for Kerala.

01

Measurement-aware forecasting

It models where a seizure gets reported and located, not just where drugs move. That took accuracy from 63% to 85%.

02

Malayalam geocoder

It reads datelines and case endings, and skips hometowns. It locates 628 seizures English sources missed.

03

Self-auditing forecast ledger

Every weekly list is locked and scored four weeks later. If v3.1 trails v1 over Dec–May, it reverts automatically.

04

Bias-resistant public tips

What and roughly where, never who. An area surfaces only when 5+ independent people report it on 2+ days, above the local norm.

05

Enforcement and care, one system

The same evidence tells police where to look and tells health planners where help is too far.

+

Recovered public data

A verified 105-month district series that wasn't available anywhere in usable form.

In the field

Who uses it, what a week looks like, and why the results can be trusted.

How it's used

A loop that checks itself every week.

Enforcement analysts
The four-week priority list, why each place, rising areas and the official district picture. They decide. Nothing is dispatched automatically.
Health & recovery planners
Drug-affected areas far from help, ranked localities and district gaps, showing where a new centre or outreach reaches the most people.
The public
Nearest help from any spot, helplines, and a safe tip form in English or Malayalam.
Why you can trust it

Every number is next to the simplest alternative.

Where it wins
Where it's weak, and we say so
A simple rule comes close. "Reported before, then most news" ties v3.1 overall. The model's real edge is first-ever places: 38% vs 25%.
New places stay hard. 9 of 43 first-ever places were caught after the cutoff.
It measures reports, not drugs. News is a selected sample: city desks are over-covered.
Calibrated, a little low. 814 reports predicted vs 889 observed after the cutoff.
Built to do no harm

Safeguards are in the code, not just the slides.

PLACES, NOT PEOPLE

No names. No houses.

Tips have no person, phone or vehicle fields. Notes are scrubbed on the server. Taps snap to 1 km before they leave the phone.

ANTI-CAMPAIGN

5 people · 3 first-hand · 2 days

Bursts are held for review. Reporters across many blocks are dropped. Each area is tested against its own neighbours (Poisson, p < 0.01).

ANTI-STIGMA

No public tip map

Notes describing people by faith, caste or origin are held out. Enforcement sees only dashed, unverified 3 × 3 km blocks.

NO FEEDBACK LOOP

Tips never train the model

Small cells under five are suppressed. Every forecast states its data date and can be withheld.

Emergencies go straight to official lines: 112 · Yodhav 99959 66666 · Excise 155358 · de-addiction 14446.
