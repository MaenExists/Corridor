# 07 — Offline forecast atlas

> Historical checkpoint from the earlier demo build. The active rebuild scope is in [project.md](../../project.md) and [the model plan](../MODEL_PLAN.md).
Run `make demo`; open http://127.0.0.1:5173. Select Wayanad or toggle Coverage, History and Documented connections.
The browser renders attributed Kerala district polygons with no online tile requests. Place search uses 84 public GeoNames localities from the companion build.
Choose 7 or 28 days, a district or fictional connection, and move the history slider. Filters affect only history.
Amber dashed outlines mean thin or unknown coverage; absence of a plotted case isn't safety. Places and connecting arcs do not identify street journeys.
Browser smoke and build checks passed with the synthetic archive. Boundaries and localities are illustrative, not official.
