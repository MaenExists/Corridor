# 10 — Leads and briefs
In `make demo`, open Leads. Submit a district and practice topic, then open the private local queue with the demo key and mark it reviewed.
In Briefs, select a district or invented known connection, save, change the assessment, and export JSON or HTML.
Browser tests saved and reviewed a lead, edited a connection brief, and checked its export was synthetic and aggregate-only.
Only structured selections are accepted: no allegations, free text, contact details or personal records. A lead can never become a cue or training row.
Exports are local downloads. Nothing allocates resources, contacts an agency or recommends a search.
