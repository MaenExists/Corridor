# District model checkpoint — 25 November 2026

- **Data.** 117 Excise monthly statements (April 2015 – September 2024) were retrieved from the Internet Archive and hashed. 105 months from January 2016 have all 14 district rows summing to the printed statewide NDPS total. Rotated totals in March 2023 and August 2022 and the scanned July 2022 page are recorded in a local manual check file. Yearly sums match the State Planning Board district series: 32 of 56 district-years exactly, the rest within 8 cases. Raids are reported only until early 2021.
- **Model.** Smoothed district share (0.3) × latest statewide total × shrunken calendar-month effect (n / (n + 4)), with negative binomial 80% intervals using the previous 24 months of errors. Chosen on 2019–2022 development; pre-registered in commit 7ea6272 before the test.
- **Test (January 2023 – September 2024).** One month ahead passed all gates: MAE 8.70 against 9.31, bias −0.1%, coverage 82.3% and rank correlation 0.81. Two and three months ahead failed skill and are withheld.
- **Live status.** The 30 June 2026 snapshot is withheld because its latest month is September 2024. As of 25 November 2026 it is withheld while rebuilding after the gap: October 2026 is recorded, and 5 more consecutive months are needed. Public uploads alone allowed a timely forecast at 2 of 70 month-ends from 2019 to 2024.
- **Next.** Ask Excise for the October 2024 onward statements (see DATA_REQUESTS). Run `make excise-snapshot` each month. Serve the 2023–2024 real replay in the interface next to the synthetic showcase.
- **Post-cutoff check (`make cutoff-check`).** Assembly answer 640 adds statewide months from January 2025 to September 2026, reconciled with the Economic Review. Cutoff issuance correctly withheld. Forced long-range district forecasts for October 2026 lost to repeating September 2024 (MAE 15.7 vs 13.1). The statewide seasonal effect underperformed a three-month mean on the four post-cutoff months (MAE 109 vs 80). A revision must be scored on November 2026 onward.
- **Where-next layer (`make where-dev`, `make where-test`).** Joint simulation of the frozen count model, pre-registered in commit 0d91198. The test passed: hot-alert precision 38% against an 18% base rate, AUC 0.76 against 0.72 for persistence, top-four Brier skill +0.41, with probabilities slightly high. Share models with spillover and seasonal terms gave no development gain. Drug-type and sub-district forecasts await range-level and drug-type aggregates.
- **Recovery access map (`make recovery-refresh`, `/recovery.html`).** 322 centres, a 1 km access grid for 34.7 million people, and ranked priority localities, led by Achankovil and Kazhuthurutty in Kollam's Punalur taluk, Ānavattom in Pathanamthitta, and villages in Vatakara, Tirurangadi and Peerumade taluks. Next: travel time, care demand by taluk once shared, and wastewater pilots.
- **Grid hotspot forecast (`make news-harvest hotspot-dev hotspot-test hotspot-audit hotspot-postcutoff hotspot-forecast`, `/hotspots.html`).** 974 located seizure reports from four publishers' sitemaps feed a learned ranker over 1 km cells. It was pre-registered in commits 909d2fd and a5510eb. The test passed all gates, with 57% of 28-day reports in the top 1% of cells against 28% for long-run hotspots, and 71% after the cutoff. The audit found the skill is mostly repeat localities: a 1 km repeat map scored within one point, and only 3% of new localities were caught. Next: official station- or range-level counts, Malayalam publishers, and revisions scored on December 2026 onward.
- **Hotspot patterns (`make context-layers patterns-evaluate patterns-forecast`).** OpenStreetMap context (stations, highways, ports, colleges, resorts, liquor outlets, police, border and 31 crossings) feeds a risk terrain, rule-labelled hotspot types, corridor spread and a learned pattern ranker. It was pre-registered in b7d840e. The ranker failed its development criterion. The risk terrain's development edge on new places did not hold against population on later windows. The relative risks are kept as descriptive context. The single-pass parallel evaluation takes about 5 minutes, against more than 20 per window before.
- **Coverage expansion.** News18 Malayalam raised located incidents to 1,262. The same-place ranker improved (72% top-1% after the cutoff against 64% for the repeat map). New-place prediction did not. Border-gateway clusters (Muthanga, Neyyattinkara, Manjeshwar) now appear.
- **Cutoff test (`make hotspot-cutoff`).** One forecast frozen at 30 June 2026 caught 123 of 162 located reports through 25 November (75.9%), against 62.3% for the repeat map and 14.8% for population. 13 of its top 20 localities had later reports. New-place catch was 1 of 23.
